3. A roll-number fallback cannot be presented as an official tie-breaker.

5. The public lookup is explicitly not identity verification.
6. Dataset provenance and source checksums are first-class fields.

## Stale assumptions to verify before implementation

- The supplied responses describe September 13, 2026 as the exam date and predict a 2–4 week result window. Treat that as a planning assumption, not an official schedule.
- `uhsrcetrank.pages.dev` is a proposed domain, not proof that the domain exists or is available.
- The current official portal may change its content and layout. Use it as a reference at implementation time, not as a source to copy.
- The final 2026 document may not include all listed CET exams or may introduce additional categories.
- The ranking hierarchy is unknown until the actual source and ordering are inspected.
