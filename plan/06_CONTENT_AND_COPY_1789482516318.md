## Methodology copy

```text
How the estimate is produced

The utility uses a published UHSR CET data source, converts it into structured candidate records, validates the records, and looks up the selected Roll Number. Where an official published order or verified ranking rule is available, the displayed position follows that evidence. Otherwise, the calculator remains unavailable or clearly labels the calculation as unverified.
```

```text
Estimated Merit Position is not the same as college allocation.

This utility does not simulate counselling, seat allocation, category-wise availability, candidate preferences, institute allotment, or admission chances. It does not tell you whether a rank is good or bad.
```

## FAQ

### Is this the official UHSR result?

No. This is an independent candidate utility. Use the official UHSR result or scorecard for counselling, verification, and every official purpose.

### Why is the calculator unavailable?

The relevant source data has not yet been processed and validated for public lookup.

### How is the estimated merit position calculated?

The method depends on the selected CET dataset and the evidence available in the published source. The result page identifies the dataset and explains the method used.

### Does this predict my college?

No. It does not predict colleges, seats, counselling outcomes, cutoffs, or admission chances.

### What if my Roll Number is not found?

Check the selected CET examination and enter the Roll Number exactly as it appears in the published source. If it still cannot be found, use the official UHSR result or scorecard.

### Can I rely on this for counselling?

No. The official UHSR result and scorecard are the documents to use for counselling and document verification.

### Is entering a Roll Number an identity check?

No. The lookup is a convenience utility, not a private authenticated portal. Do not enter another person's details or treat the site as proof of identity.

### How often is the data updated?

Only when a new source is imported, validated, and published. The result page shows the dataset version used.

### Why might a position differ from another document?

UHSR may publish revised data, separate CET datasets, or a different ordering for a different purpose. Compare the dataset version and use the official source for final decisions.

### Who operates this site?

The site is an independent candidate utility and is not operated, endorsed, or maintained by UHSR or the Department of Medical Education and Research, Government of Haryana.
