Any estimated merit position shown here is independently calculated from publicly available UHSR CET data. It is not an official UHSR rank. For counselling, document verification, and all official purposes, rely on the official UHSR result or scorecard.

How the Estimate Is Produced

Source and preparation

The utility uses a published UHSR CET data source, converts it into structured candidate records, validates the records, and looks up the selected Roll Number. The source reference, dataset version, and validation outcome are retained with the dataset.

Raw source files are not served through the public lookup. Candidate fields displayed publicly are limited to the reviewed result view.

Ordering and uncertainty

Where an official published order or verified ranking rule is available, the displayed position follows that evidence. Otherwise the calculator remains unavailable or clearly labels the calculation as unverified. The ranking configuration is versioned with each dataset.

Estimated Merit Position is not an official rank

It is an independent calculation or presentation of source order. It does not establish admission priority, counselling eligibility, seat allocation, or a college outcome.

What this utility does not do

  • It does not simulate counselling or seat allocation.
  • It does not predict colleges, cutoffs, or admission chances.
  • It does not verify a person's identity from a Roll Number.
  • It does not replace the official UHSR result or scorecard.
