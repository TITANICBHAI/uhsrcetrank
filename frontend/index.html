About This Calculator

This utility is designed to turn a published UHSR CET candidate dataset into a searchable Roll Number lookup. Where the source and ranking evidence support it, the result will show an independently calculated Estimated Merit Position.

It does not predict colleges, simulate counselling, calculate admission chances, or replace the official scorecard.
