Frequently Asked Questions

Is this the official UHSR result?No. This is an independent candidate utility. Use the official UHSR result or scorecard for counselling, verification, and every official purpose.
Why is the calculator unavailable?The relevant source data has not yet been processed and validated for public lookup. No position is invented while that gate is incomplete.
How is the Estimated Merit Position calculated?The result page identifies the dataset and method. If a published order exists, that evidence is preferred; otherwise the configured method remains clearly marked as unverified.
Does this predict my college?No. It does not predict colleges, seats, counselling outcomes, cutoffs, or admission chances.
What if my Roll Number is not found?Check the selected examination and enter the Roll Number exactly as it appears in the published source. If it still cannot be found, use the official UHSR result.
Can I rely on this for counselling?No. The official UHSR result and scorecard are the documents to use for counselling and document verification.
Is entering a Roll Number an identity check?No. The lookup is a convenience utility, not a private authenticated portal. Do not treat it as proof of identity.
How often is data updated?Only when a new source is imported, validated, reviewed, and explicitly published. The result page shows the dataset version used.
Why could a position differ from another document?UHSR may publish revised data, separate datasets, or a different ordering. Compare the dataset version and use the official source for final decisions.
Who operates this site?This is an independent candidate utility. It is not operated, endorsed, or maintained by UHSR or the Department of Medical Education and Research, Government of Haryana.
